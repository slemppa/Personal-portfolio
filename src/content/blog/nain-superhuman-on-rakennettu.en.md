What if you had a coach who could see how you slept last night, knew what
workout is waiting today, and remembered what you promised yourself last week?
One who doesn't hand out generic advice but speaks to your exact numbers?

That's what **Superhuman** is building. This is the opening post about a new
project — and at the same time a glimpse into why it's more than just one more app.

## Too many apps, too little clarity

For most of us, health is scattered. Sleep in one app, workouts in another,
nutrition in a third, and goals on some sticky note. There's more data than ever,
but **[no more understanding at all](/blog/pesa-anna-jokaiselle-eurolle-koti).** The numbers don't tell you
what you're supposed to do with them.

Superhuman starts from the opposite idea: one place that gathers the pieces
together and turns them into clear, personal advice.

## Everything in one view

Superhuman brings under one roof what is usually scattered:

- **Health** — sleep, heart rate, recovery, and steps straight from your phone's health data.
- **Training** — a smart program that adapts to how your body responds, on both
  the strength and running side.
- **Nutrition** — a lightweight food journal that keeps up without fussy micromanaging.
- **Habits and goals** — small daily actions and big directions in the same picture.

When everything is in one place, you start to see the connections: how a bad
night shows up in your training, how recovery follows your rhythm, how small
actions pile up into results.

## A coach who actually knows you

The heart of Superhuman is the **[AI coach](https://www.rayfit.com/blog/2026/02/best-ai-personal-trainer-app/)** — but not the kind that
parrots the internet's generic wisdom. It looks at your data and talks with you
based on it.

What sets it apart from an ordinary chatbot is two things: the coach **remembers**
earlier conversations and learns about you over time, and it **justifies** its
advice — you can always see which numbers or workouts it's leaning on. So it
doesn't just say "rest more," it tells you *why*, this exact week, in your exact
case.

The result feels less like an app and more like a coach who has walked the
journey with you.

## Training that adapts to you

Most programs are built for the average person. Superhuman's training is built
from your goals and updates according to how you actually feel and recover.
After a good week it raises the bar; after a demanding stretch it gives you room.

The goal isn't to do as much as possible, but **the right amount at the right time** —
so you make progress without running yourself into the ground.

## Where this is headed

Superhuman is only getting started, and I'm building it in the open. In the next
posts I'll say more about how the coach reasons, how the training program evolves
from week to week, and what I've learned along the way.

The idea is simple: to make elite coaching into something that rides in your
pocket and speaks your language. Welcome along on the journey toward the
superhuman — one step at a time.
